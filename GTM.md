# Amixos — Go-to-Market Strategy
*Last updated: Feb 2026 | Owner: Jarvis*

---

## Market Size

- **4.7 million** Hispanic-owned businesses in the US (Stanford LBAN, 2023)
- Fastest growing SMB segment — up 44% in the last decade
- **$800 billion+** in annual revenue generated by Hispanic businesses
- Only ~15% use any dedicated business management software
- That's a **~4 million business addressable market** with no dominant Spanish-first player

**Top industries (Amixos target):**
- Construction & contracting (~680K businesses)
- Landscaping & lawn care (~420K)
- Cleaning services (~380K)
- Food/catering (~290K)
- Auto repair (~210K)
- Plumbing/HVAC/electrical (~190K)

## Channel Strategy

### Phase 1 — Organic (0–3 months, $0 cost)

#### Facebook Groups (Start here — free, highest intent)
Post authentically as the founder who built this for his own business.

**Target groups (prioritized):**
1. Negocios Hispanos en USA (~150K members)
2. Empresarios Latinos USA (~80K)
3. Hispanic Business Network (~60K)
4. Latinos en los Negocios (~55K)
5. Contratistas Hispanos USA (~40K)
6. Negocios Latinos de Construcción (~35K)
7. [State-specific] Negocios Hispanos en Texas/Florida/California/Georgia/Nebraska
8. Construction-specific: Contratistas en USA, Hispanic Contractors Network
9. Landscaping: Jardineros Profesionales USA
10. Cleaning: Limpieza Profesional USA

**Post template (Spanish):**
> "Hola familia. Soy contratista — manejo dos empresas de construcción y 40 trabajadores entre Nebraska y Georgia. Cansado de usar Excel y WhatsApp para todo, construí mi propio sistema. Se llama Amixos — facturas, clientes, empleados, inventario — todo en español, desde el teléfono. Lo estoy abriendo para todos. Beta gratis. ¿Quién quiere probarlo? Deja tu correo aquí 👇 [link]"

**Why this works:** Authentic founder story. Hispanic community responds strongly to "uno de nosotros."

#### WhatsApp
- Post in any existing contractor/business WhatsApp groups you're already in
- Ask your own workers to share in their networks
- Create an Amixos WhatsApp channel for updates

#### LinkedIn
- Post the founder story in English targeting construction/contractor industry
- Tag USHCC (US Hispanic Chamber of Commerce)

#### TikTok / Instagram Reels
- 60-second screen recording of the actual app
- Caption: "Construí el software que mis trabajadores y yo necesitábamos"
- No production value needed — authentic works better for this audience
- Post 3x/week for 30 days to build organic reach

### Phase 2 — Paid (3–6 months, after 200+ signups validated)

#### Facebook/Instagram Ads
**Budget:** Start $20/day, scale winners to $100/day
**Targeting:**
- Language: Spanish
- Interests: small business, construction, landscaping, cleaning services
- Behaviors: small business owners
- Locations: TX, FL, CA, GA, NC (highest Hispanic contractor density)
- Age: 28–55

**Ad angles to test (in order):**
1. **Pain:** "¿Sigues usando Excel para tus facturas? Hay una mejor manera." (screenshot of messy spreadsheet → Amixos)
2. **Founder:** 15-second video of Edvin showing the app in Spanish
3. **Social proof:** "500 contratistas ya están en la lista" (once you have it)
4. **Demo:** Screen recording showing invoice creation in 30 seconds

**Expected CPL:** $3–8 per email signup at this targeting
**At $50/day:** 6–16 leads/day = 180–480/month

#### Google Ads (Month 4+)
Target high-intent searches:
- "software para contratistas en español"
- "programa de facturas en español"
- "app para manejar empleados construcción"
- "sistema administración negocios hispanos"

## Demo Video Strategy

**The app IS built. Record now.**

**What to record (60–90 seconds, phone screen recording):**
1. Open the app on phone
2. Show dashboard with stats (15 sec)
3. Create a client in 5 seconds (15 sec)
4. Create an invoice, add line items (20 sec)
5. Show employee payroll screen (10 sec)
6. End on dashboard: "Todo en español. Gratis durante beta."

**Tools:** Loom (free), or just screen record on iPhone/Android
**Language:** Spanish throughout
**Tone:** Casual, real — not scripted. Like you're showing a friend.
